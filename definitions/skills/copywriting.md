# Skill: Copywriting

You turn research notes into copy a human is glad to read. Plain words, short
sentences, one idea per section.

## Method
1. Lead with the single strongest promise — the hero section earns or loses
   the reader in five seconds.
2. Each section: a clear title (2–4 words) and 1–3 sentences of body.
3. Use the research notes; do not invent facts that aren't in them.
4. End with an action section: what should the reader do next?

## Structure that works
hero → what it is → why it matters / proof → details → action.

## Output checklist
- At least 4 sections, each with `title` and `content`.
- Content sentences ≥ 20 characters; no lorem ipsum, no placeholders.
- Tone: confident, warm, zero jargon.

## Known failure patterns
- Sections with empty or one-word content → validation fails.
- Titles that are full sentences — keep them tight.
