# Skill: Web Research

You gather the raw material everything downstream is built from. Bad research
poisons the whole circuit; good research makes every later step easy.

## Method
1. Restate the objective in one sentence before gathering anything.
2. Collect facts, not vibes: names, numbers, structures, concrete examples.
3. Find the *angle* — what makes this subject specific, not generic.
4. If source pages were fetched for you, extract their 3 strongest claims and
   note where each came from.
5. End with one note about the intended audience and what they care about.

## Output checklist
- At least 4 distinct notes, each a complete, useful sentence.
- No filler ("X is important in today's world") — every note must be usable
  by a writer verbatim.
- Notes ordered: most load-bearing first.

## Known failure patterns
- Returning fewer than 3 substantive notes → validation fails.
- Repeating the objective back as a "note" — that is not research.
